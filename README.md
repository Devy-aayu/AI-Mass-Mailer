# RitMailer

RitMailer is a free AI powered mass mailing service built to make client outreach easier and more personal
Instead of spending hours going through a spreadsheet finding contact details researching businesses and writing emails one by one you can simply provide your Excel or CSV file
## What RitMailer does

You give RitMailer a file containing your client or lead information
* Client names, Business names, Email addresses, Phone number, Websites, Locations, Services etc

If a website is available for a client RitMailer AI can visit and analyze the publicly available information on that website
It tries to understand what the business does what services it provides and other information that can help create a better outreach message
The AI then uses all of this information to create a personalized email for each client diffrently

## The main idea

RitMailer is not designed to send the same email to everyone
For example if your file contains three different businesses
```text
Business 1
Web development agency

Business 2
Restaurant

Business 3
Real estate company
```

RitMailer can create three different emails based on the individual businesses
The email for the web development agency can focus on their business and potential web related needs
The email for the restaurant can be written around their restaurant and the service you are offering
The email for the real estate company can be written around their real estate business and its specific needs
This makes the outreach feel much more personal than sending one generic message to every lead

## How it works
```text
Upload your CSV or Excel file
AI analyzes the file
Finds useful client information
Checks available websites
Researches the business
Understands the client and their business
Generates a personalized email
Send the emails as a campaign
```

## Example input
Your Excel or CSV file could look something like this
```csv
Name,Company,Email,Phone,Website
John Smith,ABC Agency,john@example.com,+1234567890,https://example.com
Sarah Lee,XYZ Restaurant,sarah@example.com,+1234567891,https://example.com
Mike Johnson,Prime Realty,mike@example.com,+1234567892,https://example.com
```

RitMailer analyzes the available information and uses it to understand each lead
You do not need to manually create a separate email for every person

## AI personalization

The important part of RitMailer is the personalization layer
The AI can combine information from the uploaded file with information available on the clients website
For example it may identify
```text
What the business does
What services the business provides
Who the business serves
Information about their products
Their location
Their online presence
Other publicly available information that can help personalize the message
```

The AI can then use this context when writing the email
The result is intended to feel less like a mass generated message and more like an email written specifically for that business
